01 About

I’m a passionate UX/UI and Graphic Designer with a love for visual storytelling, layout, and digital experience design, and a very bubbly, warm person to work with.

My goal is to bridge visual design and user experience to create thoughtful work made to be seen: design that enhances everyday interactions, builds meaningful connections, and turns ideas into experiences.

I’ve worked with design and marketing teams on branding, campaign design, and UX/UI projects, for clients across many industries. I’m also completing my Bachelor of Digital Experience Design at George Brown College.
